# AI Visibility

AI visibility is the degree to which a product can be accurately discovered, understood, cited, compared, and recommended by AI-mediated search and answer workflows.

AnswerLens treats AI visibility as an evidence problem, not a magic ranking problem.

## Practical signals

- Crawlable pages for product, pricing, security, docs, FAQ, integrations, compare, and use cases
- Visible copy that states who the product is for and what it does
- Citable proof such as pricing details, trust controls, docs, examples, and comparison criteria
- Structured data that agrees with visible text
- Prompt-pack evals that measure accurate mentions, citations, recommendations, and misrepresentations

## Why classic SEO is not enough

Traditional SEO still matters, but answer engines also need source material that can survive summarization. A page can be indexed and still be hard for AI systems to explain if it lacks explicit evidence, comparison language, or trustworthy page-to-page context.
