# AnswerLens vs AI SEO Dashboards

Many AI SEO dashboards are designed around hosted monitoring, marketing workflows, and cross-channel visibility views.

AnswerLens is intentionally different: it is a CLI-first, report-driven, repo-native audit tool.

## Dashboard-first tools may fit when

- A marketing team needs a hosted interface
- Stakeholders want ongoing charts and account-level views
- The organization prefers vendor-managed data storage
- Workflow lives outside GitHub and CI

## AnswerLens may fit when

- Developers, open-source authors, or DevRel teams want artifacts in the repo
- A team wants reproducible runs in CI
- Findings need to be reviewed in PRs and release notes
- The team cares about explainable rules and visible evidence
- Avoiding consumer AI UI scraping is a hard requirement

## Positioning

AnswerLens is not trying to be a prettier dashboard. It is CI for AI discoverability: a way to catch source-material regressions before they ship.
