# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in AnswerLens a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- giving constructive, actionable feedback
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- focusing criticism on ideas and implementation, not people
- assuming good intent while staying honest about problems

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- trolling or deliberately inflammatory comments
- publishing private information without permission
- any conduct that would make collaboration feel unsafe

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through the support or security channels documented in this repository.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
